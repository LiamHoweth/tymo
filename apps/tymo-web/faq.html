What is Tymo?

We’re building Tymo so customers can request a booking and pay on your website. You review the request, approve the time, and track the booking and payment in one place.

Who is Tymo for?

Tymo is for businesses that sell time or space. We’re starting with field and court rentals at sports complexes. The same booking flow can also serve studios, salons, spas, venues, and coaches.

Do my customers need to download an app?

Customers use a web browser on their phone, tablet, or computer to book directly through your website.

Will it look like my business?

Yes. Your booking page uses your business name, logo, and colors so customers recognize where they’re booking.

How does setup work?

We do the setup. You walk us through your spaces, services, hours, and staff. We use those details to build your booking page and connect it to your website. To get started, tell us about your business and request a demo.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your business. We’ll talk through it on your demo call. The demo is free, takes 30 minutes or less, and comes with no obligation.

How quickly will you get back to me?

Someone from Tymo will personally reply to your demo request within 24 hours.
